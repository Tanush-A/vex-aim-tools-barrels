# HOW-IT-WORKS, the short version — start here

One page to understand (or re-explain) the detector. The long form with worked numbers is `HOW-IT-WORKS.md`; the diagrams of how the files fit are `ARCHITECTURE.md`. State as of 2026-11-09 (the hs color model is the front end, the stripe step with hardening a–g is the detector; all on `barrels`).

**Naming note.** `EXPERIMENTS.md`, `DECISIONS.md` and `TODO.md` call the stripe step "detector step 2" (it was the second thing built, after "step 1" = blobs → count → bottom edge). In the two HOW-IT-WORKS files the detector is explained as four steps, and the stripe is **step 4**. Same thing.

## The chain

**Photo (640 × 480) → class mask → barrels → photo with boxes.**

```
python barrels/tools/detect_image.py barrels/data/raw/4A8B_window_light_touch_02.png            # the detector (steps 1–4)
python barrels/tools/detect_image.py barrels/data/raw/4A8B_window_light_touch_02.png --step1    # steps 1–3 only, for comparison
```

Nothing learns from the photo it is looking at. The only learned numbers are two per color — a hue range and a saturation floor — fit once from labeled frames. Everything else is physics: barrel size, camera height, tilt.

## 1. Color segmentation (`pipeline.py`, the hs model since 2026-11-09)

Every pixel becomes orange (1), blue (2) or other (0). A pixel is a barrel color when it is below row 50 (above the horizon nothing is a barrel), brighter than 40, its **hue** is inside the class's range and its **HSV saturation** ((max − min)/max: a shaded navy face keeps it high, a gray wall never reaches it) is above the class's floor. Orange: hue 3–13, S ≥ 140. Blue: hue 103–119, S ≥ 113. Those four numbers were read off the 16 labeled home frames once; nothing is estimated from the image being looked at. Scores: campus 0.99 vs 0.41 for the old front end, home 0.93 unchanged, nothing on any empty frame.

**What it replaced (v1, until 2026-11-09):** a saturation floor and a wall color estimated **per image** from the top 50 rows, then fixed RGB boxes. That assumed the top rows are a plain wall; in a real room (Dave's office, the campus study room) they are clutter, the floor jumped to 96–120, and the barrel bodies themselves were thrown away. Kept as `--model results/2026-10-20_v1_test_adaptive/config.json`; `HOW-IT-WORKS.md` §1, second half.

**Open question (TODO steps 8 and 10):** the thresholds are fixed, so a strong color cast can move orange out of its hue range. The plan is to look at hue/saturation histograms across light settings first, then decide what an adaptive version keys on.

## 2. Camera geometry (`geometry.py`)

Pinhole camera 43.47 mm above the table, tilted down 22.25°, focal length 400 px. Two formulas: a point at distance d and height z lands on row `240 + f·tan(atan((h − z)/d) − tilt)`, and backwards a row gives a distance. The barrel is 24 mm tall, 25.5 mm wide at the bulge, stripe 6 mm at mid-height. So for a barrel at any distance the geometry says its top row, stripe rows, bottom row and width (`barrel_rows`); and seeing any one of those tells you the distance and therefore all the others. At 20 cm: 51 px wide, 55 tall, bottom at row 174. Horizon row 76.

The tilt was corrected on 2026-11-03: the October estimate (23.3°) put every predicted bottom ≈ 20 px too low; fitting from 24 labeled barrels gives 22.25°. Labeled boxes pin the horizon row, not f and tilt separately; the known-distance calibration frames will do that.

## 3. The detector (`detector.py`)

- **Step 1, blobs.** Group touching barrel-colored pixels, orange and blue together (body + stripe stay together); close 3 px gaps; drop specks under 20 px.
- **Step 2, how many barrels.** Blob width ÷ the width one barrel must have at that row (from the bottom row, or the top row if the bottom is out of frame), rounded. Two or more → cut at the waist, the narrowest column near the halfway point, because touching barrels meet only at their bulges. Each piece's color is its majority color, the body.
- **Step 3, the bottom edge.** The top row gives the distance, the distance says where the base must be. Keep the seen bottom if it agrees within 15 % of the height (min 4 px); otherwise draw the predicted bottom and flag `bottom_inferred`. Clip to the frame; a box at the frame edge is `truncated`. Drop pieces that are not barrel-sized for their row (0.4–1.6 × the expected width, and nearer than ≈ 60 cm).
- **Step 4, the stripe (the default since 2026-11-05).** A stripe is a patch of the *other* color with body color right above *and* right below it (within 3 stripe heights, pixel by pixel — since 2026-11-08; before, it was any non-body gap between body above and below, which let glued shadow veto a stripe and let a dark gap pass as one). It must have stripe proportions for its row (0.45–1.6 × the expected stripe height, at least 0.4 barrels wide). Every stripe is one barrel: its row gives the distance, the distance the full box; seen edges are used when they agree; the box must hold body color in the stripe's columns (else it belongs to something else). A narrow stripe is partly hidden → the box is widened to a full barrel toward the occluder, flagged `occluded`. (2026-11-11, branch `wip/occlusion`, **both off / unchanged by default**: a barrel hidden at the side with under 0.4 of its stripe showing is refused by the width test — campus `occl_02`; lowering the floor to 0.25 finds it but adds 2 false boxes on wood frames, so it is not adopted; switch (h) `occl_body_side` reads the hidden side from the body color joined to the stripe. `detect(..., log=[])` lists every candidate dropped and the test that dropped it; it never changes the answer.) Pieces without a stripe survive only where a stripe could not be seen: the blob reaches the frame bottom (close barrel) or the expected stripe is under 4 px (too far) — and they must be barrel-tall as well as barrel-wide.

**2026-11-18, branch `wip/detector-given-seg`, two new parts of the detector, **on by default since 2026-11-18** (Tanush: understood; the two boxes that broke the written rule do not block it):**
- `stripe_join`: a highlight or a shadow line cuts a stripe in pieces and each piece fails alone. Pieces that share rows and together fit in one barrel's width (1.2 ×) are one stripe, because two barrels cannot both have their stripe there. A piece joins only if it is stripe-shaped (not taller than 1.6 stripe heights, not taller than wide).
- `cap_behind`: a barrel behind one of the other color shows only its top; its stripe melts into the front barrel's body. After everything else is found: a piece of one color that is barrel-wide and as tall as an upper body for its row, and whose lower part would lie inside a found barrel standing nearer, is a barrel. A narrow one must have exactly one side hidden by a found barrel or the frame edge. Box: top seen; height from the width when the whole width shows (the top row alone puts bottoms 4–7 px low, `size_check.md`); a seen bottom of its own color is used when it agrees.
- Test "good segmentation in, every barrel out" (`experiments/2026-11-18_det_given_seg`): perfect home masks 25 → 28 of 29, damaged masks 131 / 36 / 12 → 141 / 64 / 13 of 145, black table with label thresholds 17 → 19 of 26, eval table home 25 → 27 of 29 with no false box, campus unchanged. The pre-registered rule broke twice by the letter, which Tanush accepted: one box on black sits 7 px below a hand-estimated bottom, and one wrong box appears in the wood clip. `HOW-IT-WORKS.md` §3, "The detector given good segmentation".

**2026-11-19, branch `wip/detector-side`, two more parts, on by default on that branch (awaiting the walkthrough)** (`experiments/2026-11-19_det_side`):
- `side_hidden`: a stripe narrower than 0.8 of a barrel is partly seen. Before, the box was widened toward a barrel-colored pixel beside the stripe, which was nearly always the barrel's own, so a stripe cut by a highlight got a box half off the barrel; and a stripe under 0.4 of a barrel was refused outright (campus `occl_02`, the barrel behind the marker stand). Now: what is seen of the barrel is the stripe plus the body joined to it. If that shows 0.8 of a barrel, it is the box. Otherwise a side counts as hidden only for a reason: the frame edge, a found barrel standing nearer, or the body and the stripe ending at different columns there (at a barrel's own edge they end together). One side hidden → a barrel wide from the seen edge of the other. No side or both → centred if the stripe is at least 0.4 of a barrel, else no box. The finder's width floor is one stripe height.
- `seam_height`: mixed pixels at the two seams thin a far stripe below the height floor, and widen the gap between the two body parts by the same amount. A stripe too thin by its own rows, and seen whole (0.8 of a barrel), is read again as the mean of its height and of that gap.
- Numbers: campus 34 → 35 of 35 (`occl_02` box 0.82 overlap with the hand-drawn one), home unchanged 27 of 29; medium damage 64 → 99 of 145 with false boxes 22 → 1; edges eaten by 2 px 95 → 110; no home or campus row loses, nothing on a negative frame. Still missed: a barrel behind a straight upright edge with under 0.4 of its stripe showing; the wedge barrel in home `cluster_01` (not measurable: no box on purpose); the second `cluster_01` barrel (the bottom from the top row is 10 px low: calibration, TODO A4). `HOW-IT-WORKS.md` §3, "Side occlusion, and stripes eaten at the seams".

**2026-11-19, branch `wip/detector-bottom`, one small part, on by default on that branch (awaiting the walkthrough)** (`experiments/2026-11-19_det_bottom`, TODO A5):
- The question: the bottom worked out from a barrel's top row is 4–7 px low at mid and far range with this camera. Does that matter? Counted: of the 62 boxes drawn on home and campus, none takes its bottom from the top row (55 seen bottoms, 5 at the frame edge, 2 caps seen whole). It acted in one place: a **narrow cap** (a barrel behind another, one side hidden), where the top-row bottom is what a seen end is compared with.
- `cap_seen_bottom`: for a narrow cap whose seen end is refused, the cap's color is read beside the front barrel (not inside its box: there it is the front barrel's stripe). If it is seen ending between the bottom of a barrel only as wide as the seen cap (it is at least that big, whatever the tilt) and the top-row bottom plus the usual tolerance, that end is the bottom.
- Numbers: home 27 → 28 of 29 (the left orange barrel of `cluster_01`: seen end 162, top-row bottom 173, tolerance 8; box overlap 0.71 with the hand-drawn one); campus 35 of 35 unchanged; light damage 141 → 143 of 145; no row loses, no false box, nothing on a negative frame; no box changes in the 337 recorded frames. Still missed at home: the wedge barrel of `cluster_01`. `HOW-IT-WORKS.md` §3, "Hidden bottoms and the top row".

**2026-11-19, branch `wip/detector-occl`: the occlusion scorecard and one idea (awaiting the walkthrough)** (`experiments/2026-11-19_det_occl`; `results/2026-11-19_det_occl/scorecard.md`, `verdict.md`):
- **The scorecard.** Real occluded frames are too few to measure anything, so the test makes them: each of the 50 free-standing barrels of the train frames is put alone on an empty mask at its own place, and one thing hides 25, 50 or 75 % of it: the frame edge, a straight or slanted edge, both sides, from above, from below, a post, a barrel of the other or the same color (pasted, scaled for where it stands), a plain block of barrel color. 3888 scenes, scored against the barrel's full box (`occlude.py`). It works on the mask: shadows, reflections and color bleed of a real occluder are not in it.
- **The one idea:** what is seen fixes the distance (the stripe's row); the distance fixes the size; the box is the full barrel, placed where the seen part allows; where nothing pins it, the box covers every place the barrel can be. A side is moved only for a reason (frame edge, a found nearer barrel, body and stripe ending apart); with a reason on one side, the box is a barrel wide from the seen edge of the other. With none, or one on both, the barrel lies between (seen right end − W) and (seen left end + W), and that span is the box, "the cover box": if the share v of the width shows it is (2 − v) barrels wide, always holds the barrel, and overlaps its true box by 1 / (2 − v), never under half.
- **On by default** (passed the pre-registered rule): `edge_check`: an edge is seen only against something that is not a barrel. A band with neither its top nor its bottom where its row predicts them, whose body above ends against the other barrel color, is not a barrel (it was a strip of a back barrel between its own stripe and the front barrel, boxed in the front barrel's color); and a seen bottom that ends against another barrel becomes the lower of seen and predicted. `cap_same`: the top of a barrel showing above a found barrel of its **own** color is tested as a cap too (before, it was skipped as that barrel's own body).
- **Built and off** (they broke the rule by the letter): `slice_box`: a piece of stripe down to 0.45 of a stripe height wide counts, if the slice is seen from top to bottom where its row predicts; unpinned, it gets the cover box. `stripe_two`: a stripe wider than 1.2 barrels is two same-colored barrels, one at each end.
- **Numbers** (found of 3888 scenes: before → default → with the off switches): 2401 → 2417 → 3155; boxes where no barrel is 63 → 45 → 58. Three quarters hidden by a straight edge: 0 → 0 → 73 of 100; both sides: 0 → 0 → 44 of 50; same color beside, half hidden: 36 → 36 → 88 of 100; other color straight in front, a quarter hidden: 29 → 39 → 39 of 47. HOME 28 of 29 and CAMPUS 35 of 35 unchanged in every column; no recorded frame changes with the default. Why the three are off: with them, medium damage finds 124 instead of 99 of 145 but draws 4 false boxes instead of 1 (a cover box 1.8 barrels wide on a damaged barrel that is not hidden overlaps the truth by under half). Not handled by anything: hidden from above or below by half or more (no stripe with body above and below is left); a same-colored barrel straight in front hiding half or more.

**2026-11-20, branch `wip/occl-vertical`: occlusion up and down, six more switches, all on by default on that branch (awaiting the walkthrough)** (`experiments/2026-11-20_occl_vertical`; `results/2026-11-20_occl_vertical/verdict.md`, `amounts.md`):
- The rule behind all of them: a distance reading counts only with a second reading that agrees, and every box shows the barrel's whole width or is pinned by a found barrel. No side is guessed. They run last and only add boxes.
- `half_stripe` (+ `half_thin`): a barrel hidden from above shows its stripe and lower body, from below its upper body and stripe; the finder wanted body color on both sides. Now a band with body color on **one** side only is a barrel when it is a barrel wide, not taller than a stripe and thinner than the body beside it, and the body's far edge is where the **seam** between band and body predicts it (the stripe's edges are 15 mm and 9 mm above the table, so the seam alone gives the distance) and ends against something that is not barrel-colored. The hidden edge is drawn where the seam's distance puts it. `half_thin`: a few rows of stripe are enough.
- `post_pair`: two narrow stripe pieces at the same rows, each a slice seen from top to bottom, outer edges one barrel apart, are one barrel behind a post.
- `stripe_wide`: a stripe wider than 1.2 barrels is two barrels (`stripe_two`), asked only where body color is seen right above and below it; that guard removes `stripe_two`'s three false boxes.
- `slice_behind`: a narrow slice seen from top to bottom with one side against a found nearer barrel or the frame edge is a barrel, a barrel wide from its seen edge.
- `cap_low`: a top behind a found barrel counts from 0.4 of an upper body tall (0.5 before).
- **Numbers** (scorecard, found of n at 50 % hidden unless said): from above 2 → 45 of 50; from below 1 → 40; a post 9 → 41 (three quarters: 0 → 9); same color beside 36 → 88 of 100 (a quarter: 81 → 96); same color beside and lower, three quarters, 26 → 42; a barrel straight in front, three quarters, 17 → 22 of 49 (other color) and 2 → 8 (same). All 3888 scenes: 2417 → 2738 found, boxes where no barrel is 45 → 45. HOME 28 of 29 and CAMPUS 35 of 35 unchanged, nothing on a negative frame; medium damage 99 → 115 of 145 with 1 false box as before; one box appears in the 337 recorded frames, on a barrel entering at the frame edge. Report only: one wrong-color box on the 90 wood frames, one more box on Dave's.
- **Not done, because a class mask cannot say:** three quarters hidden from above or below by something that is not a barrel (a top or a base alone looks like any orange or blue thing of that size: such pieces sit on two negative frames and on hands); a same-colored barrel straight in front hiding three quarters; a same-colored barrel beside at the same rows hiding three quarters.
**Where is a cut barrel, sideways? (2026-11-20, branch `wip/occl-pin`; `pin_outline` on by default there, `pin_thin` off; awaiting the walkthrough.)** The cover box stays off, so the question became where the barrel is. Measured first (`results/2026-11-20_occl_pin/shape_census.md`): the stripe's row gives the distance, the distance gives the barrel's outline; its arcs are 1 to 2 px and useless, its corners are 10 to 17 px on a 55 px barrel: a barrel's own side has rounded corners, a cut side square ones. The rule (`_outline_place`): for a partly seen stripe that no side reason places, each end of what is seen is asked by itself whether its top corner, stripe and bottom corner all follow the outline (within `pin_good` = 0.05 of the barrel's height, over half a lid's width from the end). Exactly one such end: the box is one barrel wide from that end. Otherwise as before. *HOME `core_02`, orange (368, 120, 418, 173), left half hidden by a straight edge:* the cut end is 4.6 rows off the outline, the barrel's own end 1.8 (limit 2.7): box (368, 120, 418, 172), overlap 0.98; before, centred: (380, 120, 431, 172), overlap 0.59. Scorecard: straight edge at 50 % hidden 82 → 90 of 100, slanted 154 → 170 of 200; all scenes 2417 → 2445 found, boxes where no barrel is 45 → 45, nothing else changes anywhere (eval table, damaged masks, all recorded frames). `pin_thin` (off) lets a thin slice with one own end be a barrel: three quarters hidden by a straight edge, the frame or a block 0–5 → 28–36 of 100, also with no false box; off because the rule committed to the slanted edge at 75 %, which stays 0. Not handled by anything: a piece from the middle of a barrel with both sides cut (no cue exists in the mask), and an own side whose corner is ragged or shadowed (a miss, not a wrong box).

**Hardening a–g (2026-11-08/09)**, each a `DetConfig` switch: a dark gap is not a stripe; the stripe is measured from its own pixels; a box must hold body color; a stripeless piece must be barrel-tall; the frame edge counts as a hidden side; the box is as wide as the body joined to the stripe (not just the stripe); a stripe barrel covers a stripeless piece only if they share rows (a far barrel above a close-up must not hide it). `HOW-IT-WORKS.md` §3, "The hardening".

**Numbers** (v2 rule, train frames; `EXPERIMENTS.md` 2026-11-08 later, 2026-11-09): campus **0.99** (v1 front end 0.41), home **0.93**, nothing drawn on any negative of either session; median box width / true width 0.96. **Works:** single barrels at any distance; touching rows, same or mixed colors; bunched barrels that each show a stripe; a partly hidden barrel whose stripe shows (full box through the occluder); close barrels with the bottom below the frame, even under a far barrel; hands; empty frames; barrels cut by the frame side; the campus room, the laptop lid, the window behind.

## 4. Scoring (`eval_det.py`)

A predicted box matches a true box if same color and overlap (intersection over union) ≥ 0.5; greedy, each true barrel used once. Precision = hits ÷ boxes drawn; recall = hits ÷ barrels; F1 blends them; the headline is the mean of the orange and blue F1. The v2 rule (`--rule v2`) adds "bottom edge within a tenth of the box height" and counts every barrel, occluded ones included; a box on a clean empty frame disqualifies.

**The one table (`report.py`, `tools/report.py`, 2026-11-11).** `python barrels/tools/report.py` answers "what is better where" on one screen. Rows: each data set (HOME, CAMPUS, DAVE\*, and any new `{robot}_{light}_{surface}` session that has box labels, by itself) broken down by scenario tag, with a subtotal per set. Columns, for a **candidate** and a **baseline** side by side and then their difference: barrels found / missed, false boxes, median box width ÷ true width, median bottom-edge error in px; on the subtotal rows the headline score and the false boxes on empty frames (`DQ` = on a clean one). An arm is a color-model file plus `DetConfig` switches, so any change can be compared with today: `--cand-det dark_stripe=true --base-model default`. Defaults: today (hs + `DetConfig()`) vs the v1 front end with the same detector. It only presents — every number is `eval_det`'s v2 rule. Train frames only; it refuses test frames unless `--split test` is typed. HOME is scored leave-one-scene-out (the color model learned from those frames, so each scene is segmented by a model re-fit without it); the other sets use the model file as it is. A full `pytest` run prints the same table at the end (`BARRELS_NO_REPORT=1` skips it). Note: v1 + today's detector scores 0.39 on campus; the published 0.41 is v1 + the 2026-11-05 detector (`experiments/2026-11-11_eval_report/run.py` prints both).

## 5. On the robot (`live/`, `worldmap_bridge.py`, `tracker.py`)

Two small aim_fsm state machines on one base (`live/barrel_live.py`). **`BarrelViewer`**: once a second, take `robot.camera_image`, hand it to `BarrelDetectorHook.view`, show the result with Dave's `imshow`; every frame is answered on its own, exactly as a photo is. **`BarrelTracker`**: five times a second, the same detector, but a barrel is shown only once it has been seen in 3 of the last 4 frames, stays through a missed frame and is forgotten after 4 misses in a row (`tracker.py`; the box drawn is the median of the recent ones), so one-frame blips never show and a barrel lost for a frame does not blink. The hook converts RGB → BGR, runs sections 1 and 3, draws with `detector.draw`, converts back. At the `C>` prompt: `tm seg` / `tm boxes` / `tm both` switch the view. `tm seg` (since 2026-11-11): the main window dims every pixel that is not classified orange or blue to 30 % brightness (barrel-colored pixels stay as the camera gave them), and a second window, `<title> seg`, shows only the classified pixels, in their class color on black; `tm both` is the same with the boxes on the main window; `tm boxes` closes the second window (`wb.seg_views`, `hook.seg_only`, `BarrelLive.show`). Also: `tm save` / `tm rec` write raw frames to `live/snapshots/` for importing, `tm raw` (tracker only) overlays the per-frame boxes in white. Run: `cd barrels/live && simple_cli BarrelViewer` or `simple_cli BarrelTracker` (`RUNBOOK.md`). `BARRELS_MODEL=<results config.json>` in the environment swaps the front end (e.g. v1 for a side-by-side).
